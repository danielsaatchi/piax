Director of Institute

Daniel Saatchi, PhD

Founder & Director · PIAX

Research Professor of Computer Science & Mechanical Engineering
[4] Intelligent Systems Designs, AI & Brain-Interface Metamaterials

>/ GitHub >/ Google Scholar >/ Website

Daniel Saatchi is the founder of the Posthumanism Institute of AI & Xenobiotechnology (PIAX) and a research professor behind its eight research divisions. He holds a former postdoctoral research fellowship position — which he left — in AI and Biotechnology under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) COFUND programme, one of Europe's most competitive research frameworks, based in Spain.

After leaving MSCA, he became the founding editor of AI Journal of Arts & Metascience (AIJAM), establishing a new research topic at the intersection of brain interface metamaterials, artificial intelligence, and the arts.

His research spans intelligent systems design, metamaterials informatics, and brain interface metamaterials.

>/ Careers

Founding Research Professors

PIAX is establishing its first generation of Founding Research Professors. The first five will form the scientific founding group of the Posthumanism Institute of AI & Xenobiotechnology and will have the opportunity to establish independent research programs within the institute.

>/ Independent PI Model

PIAX does not initially employ the first five Founding Research Professors on a fixed institutional salary. Each professor is expected to develop and finance their own research program through external research funding, grants, fellowships, sponsored research, company contracts, collaborative R&D agreements, and other legitimate research income.
